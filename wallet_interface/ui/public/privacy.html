211 AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 211 AI and Abby, our AI-assisted service navigation and document wallet experience, collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information.

Short version: we use information to help people find services, manage a private wallet, communicate with providers when requested, and operate the website. We do not sell personal information. We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer SMS opt-in data or messaging consent information to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

1. Who We Are

211 AI provides a public service navigation website, an Abby AI assistant, document wallet tools, and messaging/voice features that can help people organize information and connect with social-service providers, shelters, benefits programs, and related support resources.

Contact: ops@211-ai.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Contact information, such as phone number, email address, or preferred contact method.
  • Messages, calls, voice input, transcripts, and delivery metadata when you text, call, or chat with Abby.
  • Service navigation information, such as searches, saved services, service plans, reminders, notes, interactions, and provider handoff actions.
  • Wallet information, including wallet identifiers, encrypted records, encrypted metadata, grants, revocations, audit events, proof receipts, import/export descriptors, and storage references.
  • Documents or files you choose to upload, such as identity, benefits, housing, eligibility, or case-management documents.
  • Location information only when you choose to provide it, including city, ZIP code, coarse location, or precise location for a specific wallet or service-matching purpose.
  • Provider or shelter workflow information, such as contact requests, case-management notes, proof review status, or staff messages.
  • Technical information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, error logs, security events, cache state, and API request metadata.

3. SMS, Voice, and Messaging Consent

If you provide your phone number, text our number, ask us to contact you, or otherwise opt in to messaging, you agree that 211 AI may send informational messages related to your request, including replies from Abby, service navigation follow-ups, check-in reminders, provider contact updates, and wallet or account notices. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP, CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, or STOPALL. You may reply HELP for help. Opting out of SMS may limit our ability to respond by text, but it will not prevent you from using non-SMS features that remain available.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except aggregators and providers required to deliver the messaging service, comply with law, prevent abuse, or provide the service you requested.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Answer public 211-style questions and help you find service options.
  • Create, operate, secure, recover, and delete document wallets.
  • Encrypt, store, retrieve, classify, summarize, and organize records you choose to add to your wallet.
  • Create privacy-preserving summaries, metadata, vector profiles, redacted GraphRAG outputs, and proof receipts.
  • Share selected information with service providers, shelters, emergency contacts, or other recipients only when you request or authorize that sharing.
  • Send and receive SMS, voice calls, emails, provider messages, check-ins, reminders, and service handoff messages.
  • Operate fraud prevention, abuse prevention, rate limiting, debugging, service reliability, security, audit, and compliance workflows.
  • Produce analytics only when authorized and only under reviewed aggregation, cohort threshold, suppression, privacy-budget, and audit controls.

5. Wallet Encryption, UCANs, and Proofs

Wallet records are designed so raw record payloads and sensitive metadata remain encrypted in wallet storage. Access to private records is controlled by wallet authority, grants, signed wallet invocation tokens, and revocation checks. A grant for analysis does not automatically grant plaintext decryption.

Where supported, Abby can use privacy-preserving proof receipts and zero-knowledge-style workflows to show limited claims, such as service-area membership, distance thresholds, or document category metadata, without revealing the underlying document, exact address, proof witness, or private key material. Some deployments may use deterministic or simulated proof backends for testing; production proof systems require separate verifier configuration and launch review.

6. IPFS, Filecoin, and Content-Addressed Storage

When IPFS or Filecoin-style storage is enabled, wallet data is intended to be encrypted before it is stored or pinned. CIDs, filenames, storage paths, gateway URLs, and deal IDs are not privacy controls by themselves. We treat them as sensitive metadata and use wallet encryption, access controls, retention policies, and unpin/delete workflows to reduce risk.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information:

  • With service providers, shelters, benefits agencies, emergency contacts, police precincts, social workers, or other recipients that you select or authorize.
  • With infrastructure vendors that help us operate hosting, messaging, voice, email, AI inference, storage, security, monitoring, and support systems.
  • With Twilio and telecommunications providers as needed to send and receive SMS and voice communications.
  • With AI service providers when a feature requires server-side speech, language, embedding, classification, or voice processing.
  • When required by law, legal process, safety obligations, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, or to protect rights and security.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

8. AI Processing

Abby may use local browser models, wallet-scoped routers, or hosted AI services to understand questions, retrieve public service information, transcribe voice, generate voice responses, summarize records, classify documents, or generate redacted metadata. Private wallet context should be used only when you request it or approve a workflow that needs it. Public 211 information and private wallet facts are handled as separate categories.

9. Analytics and Differential Privacy Controls

We do not approve arbitrary raw analytics queries over wallet documents, precise locations, direct identifiers, private notes, provider conversations, or raw contribution values. Production analytics must use reviewed templates, consent, nullifier checks, aggregation thresholds, suppression, query-budget accounting, privacy-budget controls, and audit events.

10. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain wallet recovery and audit history, comply with legal obligations, prevent abuse, resolve incidents, and honor retention choices. Wallet deletion and record deletion are designed to revoke grants, rotate keys when needed, remove manifest references, delete or unpin encrypted blobs where supported, and retain only audit-safe tombstones or evidence required by policy.

Content-addressed networks and recipient downloads may limit our ability to delete every copy that was already cached, pinned, or downloaded outside our control. Revocation stops future access through our wallet controls, but it cannot claw back plaintext already disclosed to an authorized recipient.

11. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, including encryption, access controls, grants, revocations, audit logs, redaction, secret management, and operational review. No service can guarantee perfect security.

12. Your Choices

  • You may choose what wallet records to create, upload, share, export, or delete.
  • You may revoke supported grants and access requests.
  • You may opt out of SMS by replying STOP or an equivalent opt-out keyword.
  • You may contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or support for your information.
  • You may avoid uploading sensitive documents or precise location unless a feature needs them.

13. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us.

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The updated version will be posted at this page with a new date.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, SMS opt-out support, wallet access requests, or deletion requests, contact ops@211-ai.com.
